Works together

One house, not a pile of apps

These are examples of how systems can coordinate. Not every device supports every action, and we will not claim that they do. The point is a home that can take a few meaningful states—not fourteen separate remotes.

Example

Movie Night

A single request that can lower shades, dim Lutron lighting, power the TV and source, bring up Sonos or the soundbar, settle nearby lamps, and optionally ease the climate—so the room is ready to watch.

Example

Goodnight

Common lights off, a check that exterior doors are closed, shades to a nighttime position, thermostats to a sleep setpoint, TV and audio off, selected exterior lighting on, and the house in a nighttime state.

Example

Away

Selected lights off, climate to an away setpoint, shades as you prefer for privacy or sun, entertainment stopped, door and window status available, and camera notification behavior that matches an empty house.

How projects often take shape

Foundation, Integrated, Signature

These are possibilities—not rigid packages, SKUs, or one-price bundles. Homes start at different points and grow. We will map a scope to what you need, not sell a canned kit.

Scope 1 Foundation

A network and lighting backbone you can live on

Structured networking, managed whole-home Wi-Fi, a proper rack, smart lighting, and basic automation. The house becomes reliable and controllable without taking on every system at once.

  • Designed Wi-Fi, switching, and equipment-room fundamentals
  • Thoughtful lighting control and a few everyday scenes
  • Room to add audio, cameras, and shades later without rework
Scope 2 Integrated

The rooms start behaving as one home

Networking plus lighting, Sonos, cameras, climate, shades, and a unified dashboard. Cross-system scenes become practical because the pieces are installed as a stack, not as leftover apps.

  • Audio, Protect-style cameras, climate, and motorized shades as first-class systems
  • Home Assistant (or equivalent) as the homeowner-facing layer
  • Documentation so the investment stays understandable
Scope 3 Signature

Architectural lighting, distributed A/V, indoor and out

Advanced lighting including Ketra where it belongs, automated shades throughout, distributed AV, indoor/outdoor entertainment, advanced scenes, centralized equipment, and a custom control interface tailored to the household.

  • Invisible technology: racks, pathways, and finishes that disappear
  • Property-wide coverage—Wi-Fi, cameras, audio, and lighting
  • Scenes and UI designed around how this family actually lives
