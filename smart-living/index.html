Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be building a new home to work with you?

No. We work on both new construction and existing homes. Many of our projects start with a single frustration, like weak Wi-Fi or too many remotes, and grow from there.

How is JackNat different from a TV installer or security company?

We're a design and integration firm. Instead of installing individual devices, we plan how all of your home's technology should work together, then design, install, commission and support it as one system.

Can you work with equipment I already have?

Often, yes. During your consultation we'll look at what you have, what's worth keeping, and how it can fit into a simpler, more integrated setup.

What happens after installation?

We walk you through everything at handoff and stay available as your local technology partner. Annual service agreements are available for homeowners who want ongoing care and check-ins.

How much does a project cost?

Every home and every plan is different, so we don't publish set prices. After your consultation, we'll provide a clear proposal based on your home and your priorities.
