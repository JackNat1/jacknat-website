About JackNat Consulting

Technology should be designed, integrated, documented, and supported as an engineered system—whether it runs a plant, an office, or a home.

The story

Enterprise depth. Residential craft. Same standard.

JackNat Consulting, LLC brings roughly 25 years of ERP consulting, implementation, solution architecture, and project leadership—especially in manufacturing with DELMIAWorks / IQMS—together with residential connected-home work that feels simple to live in.

Plant-floor systems and whole-home technology both fail the same way: when products are piled on without architecture, documentation, or someone accountable for the seams. JackNat’s differentiator is taking responsibility for making the pieces work together.

Engagements are positioned at solution architect / senior technology lead altitude—platform-agnostic at the consulting level, with deep product expertise where it matters most.

How we show up

Principles that show up in the work

Architecture before apps

Requirements, topology, and boundaries first. Configuration and gadgets second.

Own the seams

Integrations, VLANs, cutovers, and vendor handoffs are where projects fail—those are where we lean in.

Document for the next person

Topology, addressing, rack layouts, and process notes so systems remain operable without tribal knowledge.

Transfer knowledge

Training on ERP data structures, SQL, manufacturing concepts, and home systems so clients depend less on outsiders over time.
